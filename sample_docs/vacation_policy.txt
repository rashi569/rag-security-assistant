Company Vacation Policy - Effective 2026

All full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid vacation per year, accrued
monthly at a rate of 1.25 days per month. Vacation requests must be
submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal and require
manager approval.

Unused vacation days may be carried over into the following year, up to a
maximum of 5 days. Any days beyond that cap are forfeited at year-end
unless local law requires otherwise.

Employees who leave the company will be paid out for any unused, accrued
vacation days as part of their final paycheck, in accordance with state law.

Sick leave is tracked separately from vacation time and is not subject to
the same carryover cap - see the Sick Leave Policy document for details.
